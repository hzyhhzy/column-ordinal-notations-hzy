1. First root scan: actual natural identities, not OPEN substitution
-------------------------------------------------------------------
For R itself the forest before scanning is unmarked. The original frozen
scan only native-completes selected medium rows; unselected long step-1
rows are ineligible and quiet. Newly born families are skipped by that
same scan, so no inherited-mark completion occurs.

Each native family's row owners are the same actual F AT BIRTH. The
explicit medium-family formula makes every born mark a direct accurate
trace (y,delta), with delta=p(y). Therefore its word is J_y=F and its
natural cutoff is exactly theta_(y+1); carrier and factor are globally
equal before extraction. This proves the stronger natural certificate
directly and does not identify it with equality on an open source rank.

The forest selects the entire native family at every selected raw row.
Thus for each selected carrier r and its mark y, both y and y+1 are
selected. Its paired source is at least 3. The selected final row and
its implicit cap are retained. Section 4 below proves the subsequent
ordinary endpoint repairs also preserve the natural mark identities.

The result is an actual NATURAL certificate for the exact original R[k].
After repair, a top carrier need not have the same GLOBAL owner as its
earlier family members. Subsequent arguments use membership congruence,
not a false continuation of global equality.

2. Second forest: an all-index all-high induction
------------------------------------------------
At R[k], active minimum a=2; all marks are direct and their paired
sources delta satisfy delta>=3>a. Initial whole parallel packets are
the last two COMPLETE native families (k>=3). Each marked head and its
physical successor lie in the same family, so all natural data are
transported together when a packet is duplicated.

Maintain: every direct mark in the current active source band has
paired source delta>=a. Let P be the copy pivot and y the mark head.

If y>=P, the factor row is copied, and this is the original filter's
all-high branch. The new trace remains direct. If y<P, then y<a is
impossible because delta<y and delta>=a. The only other branch is
mixed/high, and the legacy guard would require f(delta)<=a. But
  f(delta)>=f(a)>a,
so that branch rejects it. The paper guard rejects it as well.
Consequently every RETAINED copied mark is all-high, with no surviving
legacy/paper discrepancy in this macro.

The new active source band consists entirely of the newly copied rows,
and its controller minimum is a'=f(a). Its retained paired sources are
delta'=f(delta)>=a'. This proves the invariant for every prescribed
number of raw stages, not just a fixed small index.

For short copying a retained marked source carrier r lies in [P,e),
and P<=y<r<e. Hence y and y+1 are physically copied. Translation on
this band gives f(y+1)=f(y)+1. Both the factor owner and the natural
cutoff are transported by the same actual controller E:
  J'_(f(y))=E(J_y),
  theta'_(f(y)+1)=E(theta_(y+1)).
Application congruence therefore gives the desired natural identity.
There is no mixed three-bound comparison to establish for this family.

The all-n quiet theorem for R[k] means the later frozen scan has no
native records and no effective mark completion. Thus this natural
certificate is not exposed to an unproved nonquiet scan in the second
initializer. The output remains the exact ORIGINAL R[k][n], including
legacy marks; we have not changed its definition to the paper variant.

3. Selected factor-successor closure survives the entire second forest
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
The invariant is SC:
  selected carrier r with a retained mark y => y,y+1 are selected.

The initial selected prefix is contiguous. Each remaining live packet
is a transported complete packet whose internal marks have their heads
inside it. Selecting a whole active packet except its controller keeps
y+1 whenever it keeps carrier r, since y+1<=r<controller. The final
packet minus the final cut controller has the same property.

For new selected copies, the source carrier is already selected. The
all-high proof gives P<=y<r<e. By SC its source y+1 is selected and also
inside the source band. Both copied rows are consequently selected, and
f(y+1)=f(y)+1 proves SC in physical coordinates. It is not enough merely
to see an apparent adjacency after renumbering; this calculation supplies
the actual physical adjacency needed for the natural cutoff.

This induction does not require every selected set to be convex. It uses
complete live packets and copying the selected source rows, exactly as
in the established raw-forest selection procedure.

4. Natural extraction: exact local criterion and repair proof
------------------------------------------------------------
For a direct mark y, natural Delta is theta_(y+1). With fixed original
actual named points, this cutoff stays the SAME ORDINAL after extraction
if and only if the physical next point y+1 is selected. Otherwise its
new selected successor is strictly higher. This is a precise necessary
and sufficient criterion for PRESERVING THE CUTOFF, not a claim that
natural semantics can never survive a larger cutoff by another argument.

SC ensures factor row y and its implicit cap y+1 are retained. Thus the
factor owner needs no endpoint repair, and Delta remains unchanged.
If carrier r's old cap c=theta_(r+1) must move to c'>=c, use the existing
same-Iter actual transporter G with crit(G)=c, G(c)=c', replacing its
owner F by G(F). Ordinary edges remain correct. Separately, the standard
application MEMBERSHIP identity says
  G(F) equiv_c F.
Since y<r, Delta=theta_(y+1)<=theta_r<c. Thus
  G(F) equiv_Delta F equiv_Delta J_y.
This proves the required natural identity. Any row used as a selected
factor has its successor selected, so it cannot simultaneously require

This is the missing stronger justification. The previous OPEN repair

whose owners cease to be globally equal to earlier native-family owners.

9. Precise rejection of the original fixed-[1] shortcut
------------------------------------------------------
The parent's suggested prefix-visit shortcut would compare the current
fixed short-M process to repeated original displayed [1]. Even wherever
the same-mask prefix comparison is valid, its resulting lower-bound
witness has a severe uniform limitation: original [1] never increases
row count. It native-completes the one copied source row, then truncates
back to the old row count; a successor/failure step removes a row.

For an ordinary well-formed state of at most B rows, each row r chooses
its core/mark data from at most 3^(r+1) possibilities (absent, unmarked,
marked at each address), and its positive step from at most r+1 values.
Overcounting harmlessly, the total possible states is at most
  sum_(h=0)^B product_(r=1)^h ((r+1)*3^(r+1))
  <= 2*(B+1)!*3^(B*(B+3)/2).
If the deterministic fixed-[1] run terminates, it has no repeated state,
so its length is bounded by this explicit elementary expression. This
does not require any comparison of ordinal sizes.

For the present two-index family the quiet-copy formula is explicit:
  |R[k]|=(k^2+k+8)/2,
  pivot(R[k])=5+(k-2)(k-3)/2,
  |R[k]|-pivot=3k-4,
  |R[k][n]|=(k^2+k+8)/2+(n-1)(3k-4)-1, k,n>=3.
In particular for k=n=x,
  B=|S|=(7x^2-13x+14)/2.
Thus the fixed-[1] witness from S has an upper bound 2^O(x^4), uniformly
in x, whenever it terminates. It cannot by itself supply the desired
high-hierarchy comparison with only a small uniform level loss.

This is NOT an upper bound on the real paper short-M runtime: short M
can increase row count. Nor does it say that S's ordinal is small. It
only rules out using the bounded-width fixed-[1] descent as a sufficiently
strong runtime witness. The original [1] versus Q2 prefix relation also
needs separate care at full-copy-domain failure, because [1] checks only
its one copied source row whereas Q2 prechecks the entire full copy.

The minimal remaining quantitative interface is therefore an unbounded-
width carrier clock, not a better constant in the fixed-[1] comparison:
after every simulated true successor its residual carrier must pay the
next input, and after every target limit it must supply the whole required
copy interval with the correct mark policy. No such uniform carrier
invariant for S and an original root close to R has been established by
this entry audit.
