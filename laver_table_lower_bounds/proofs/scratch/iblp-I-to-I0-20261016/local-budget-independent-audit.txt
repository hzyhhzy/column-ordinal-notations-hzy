1. Algebra sections 3--6: no local endpoint defect found
-------------------------------------------------------
The prefix-preserving product is correct: an owner of critical point beta
fixes alpha, and sends an alpha-root line from beta to nu_(i-1) to another
alpha-root line from nu_(i-1) to nu_i. Appending doubles its length and
keeps the entire initial m-line. Removing exactly that prefix therefore
gives R(m)=m(2^m-1) on (alpha,gamma,delta), not on the old triple.

For a FULL row s (implicit cap included), the three source addresses
c=s[0], p=s[-L-2], e=s[-L-1] and first image a=s[L] satisfy
  J(theta_c)=theta_a,
  J(theta_p)=theta_n,
  J(theta_e)=theta_(n+1).
Hence the transported chain has root theta_a and its endpoint intervals
are exactly those needed for finite auxiliary E. The row-count recurrence
2^t(n-a)+(a-1) correctly starts at cut(P), whose row count is n-1.

For I, the data (c,p,e,a)=(2,3,4,4) are correct. E4 preserves the first
three rows and copies the fourth row; the original fourth row already
accounts for the first application of f(x)=x2^x. Thus r=1,m=1 gives
f(f(1))=8 on (theta2,theta3,cap), not a global 8-line on the old base.

For the new terminal FULL row (0,1,2,3,d,d+1):2, the source triple for
the next auxiliary E is (theta0,theta2,theta3). Its first image is theta2.
The second base row gives the required four-point owner, so R(m)-linedness
of this source triple follows directly from the original global m-line.
The E2 round count is R(m), while the numerical iteration starts at m.
These parameters are not interchangeable.

2. Important address distinction in full-iblp section 3
-------------------------------------------------------
The original RAW forest packet starts at
  e_forest = core[-L],
where core omits the implicit final cap. That formula matches the old
raw-forest lemma. It is NOT generally the owner's first image s[L].

For the same six-point row:
  FULL s=(0,1,2,3,d,d+1), CORE=(0,1,2,3,d), L=2;
  auxiliary-E first image s[L] = 2;
  original RAW-forest packet start core[-L] = 3.

Therefore full-iblp section 3 should call e_forest the forest packet's
start/central tail boundary, not the "first central image". Its subsequent
whole-packet resource (theta_e_forest, old implicit cap, C) is the appropriate
one for that packet. It must not be replaced by the auxiliary-E source
triple or by the line on (theta2,theta3,cap) produced in algebra section 5.

The forest lemma needs the COMPLETE packet through its implicit cap. The
section-3 resource correctly includes that extra endpoint. Its actual mark
safety and scan/extraction conditions are stated as additional hypotheses,
not inferred from a large line length. Keep those conditions explicit.

3. Scope of f_new and the every-step requirement
-----------------------------------------------
The added successor-type clause
  f_new(P,m) = (x -> f_new(cut(P),x))^[2^R(m)](m)
is a legitimate CONDITIONAL extension of the existing natural-certificate
numerical theorem, provided precisely that theorem's E copying closure,
arbitrary decorated-realization double-block extraction, and decreasing
finite-table potential are retained. Its E2 geometry is the same one used
there. No monotonicity of the inner numerical function is needed: a larger
actual line can be directly weakened to the claimed exact lower bound.

This is not a proof for every naked legacy-IBLP realization. The original
long-mark semantic closure remains missing. Nor does it prove f_new>=f_star
pointwise; adding/replacing a recursion branch does not imply that order.

Most importantly, retaining the old transient branch still permits long
runs of ordinary M in other shapes. The new E2 clause provides an explicit
next jump for THIS terminal type; it does not satisfy the user's new demand
for every original step throughout R's cone. A complete compiler must give
an a-priori cost bound and update budget for each original step, without an
unknown wait until some later successor/limit shape.

4. General-root critical counting is allowed
--------------------------------------------
Qi v5 Lemma 2.5 explicitly says that ANY m-lined triple theta0,theta1,theta2
has theta2>=gamma_(2^m), with witnesses in the same Iter(j). It does not
restrict the first two points to kappa0,kappa1. Thus algebra section 7's
q(5)>2^Y from a local Y-line ending strictly below kappa4 is supported.
Some older notes used only the weaker distinct-point count for local roots;
that was conservative, not an obstruction to the exponential counting here.

5. Direct weakening implementation
----------------------------------
For adjacent segment owners U:r->a->b and V:r->b->c, the owner
V(V)(U) has critical point r and edges r->a->c. Exactly m-k merges weaken
an m-line to a k-line at unchanged endpoints. This avoids an unsupplied
critical-endpoint-map search under the new directness requirement. Added
this operational version to local-budget-hardy-contract.txt section 1.

Verdict: local algebra and direct E4/E2 interfaces are sound under their
stated old semantic hypotheses. Fix the misleading packet-start label.
Do not promote the numerical f_new theorem to an original-R every-step
compiler, nor promote newly large local linedness to the wrong triple.
No new q(5)>=H_R result follows yet. No experiment or background process.
