The OPEN extraction lemma is used only to locate the ordinary endpoint
repair, not to prove the stronger membership assertion. That assertion
is proved separately in section 4 below by the standard application
membership identity G(F) equiv_(crit G) F.

1. Exact natural invariant, and the special direct-mark case
-----------------------------------------------------------
For a proper mark y of carrier row r, let its actual p-trace be
  (v0=y,v1,...,v_(l-1)=t,delta),
and W=J_v0 o ... o J_t. Its natural cutoff is
  Delta(W)=(J_v0 o ... o J_v_(l-2))(theta_(t+1)).
The required invariant is the MEMBERSHIP equality
  J_r equiv_Delta(W) W.
For a direct mark its trace is simply (y,delta), hence
  W=J_y, Delta=theta_(y+1).                         (1.1)

At an ordinary native birth, all members of the new native family carry
the same actual owner F. The newly created marks are direct and refer
only to earlier members of that same contiguous native family. Thus
carrier=F=factor globally, and (1.1) holds without any cutoff issue.

After extraction it is NOT necessary that the global owners remain equal:
a top carrier can have its endpoint repaired while a factor does not.
From that point onward retain membership (1.1), not global owner identity.

2. All retained marks in the second forest are direct ALL-HIGH copies
--------------------------------------------------------------------
For R[K], K>=3, the active controller minimum is coordinate a=2.
Every mark is native-born and direct. If its paired source is delta,
then delta>=3>a. These facts follow from the triangular native family
formula: the fixed first five rows are unmarked, and new marked targets
have their paired sources in the chain beginning 3,4,5,... .

More generally suppose every direct mark in the active copied source
has paired source delta>=a, the current controller minimum. Let P be
the current copy pivot. For its head y, delta<y. The original legacy
filter has these alternatives:

  * y>=P: the nonterminal factor row y is fully copied, so the mark
    remains direct and its owner/factor are transported by the same E;
  * y<P and y<a: impossible, since delta<y and delta>=a;
  * a<=y<P: the high legacy branch can keep it only if the transformed
    paired source f(delta)<=a. But strict increase on the defined row
    domain gives f(delta)>=f(a)>a. Hence this branch is rejected.

Consequently EVERY retained copied mark is an all-high direct mark.
In particular no retained mark depends on the disputed difference between
the legacy high guard and the paper guard. Both guards give the same
retained marks in this source family.

The next active source interval consists of newly copied rows. Its new
minimum is a'=f(a), and every retained paired source satisfies
  delta'=f(delta)>=f(a)=a'.
All traces remain direct because both the factor row and its p endpoint
are transported by the commuting raw address map. Thus the induction
continues for any prescribed finite number of raw copies, not only for
the small initial coordinate a=2.

For the SHORT forest this source assertion has a separate direct check:
the current parallel live controllers have the same low pivot P. The
newly appended short-copy block begins at the old last-row address
f(P)=old N, and the next surviving controller's pivot is also f(P).
Therefore its next source interval starts at that new block, rather
than accidentally reaching back into the frozen old prefix. This is
not an inference merely from the analogous full-copy property.

The argument applies to the actual short-copy forest too. Its source is
[P,e), where e is the active whole-packet start. A copied mark has
  P<=y<r<e,
so its factor and the physical next point y+1 are both in the copied
interval. On physical source indices the map is translation, including
the boundary at e. Thus its natural cutoff is transported EXACTLY:
  theta'_(f(y)+1)=E(theta_(y+1)).                   (2.1)
Application congruence transports the old natural membership certificate
to the new one. No OPEN-to-membership inference and no high-branch
three-bound argument is needed here.

Initial parallel packets preserve this starting invariant: they comprise
complete native families, their marks are internal, and their low paired
sources still satisfy delta>=a. Their actual E duplication transports
carrier, factor, and successor point together. Therefore they carry
genuine natural membership certificates before the raw forest starts.

3. The forest's selection is factor-successor closed
----------------------------------------------------
Let Sigma be the selected physical row indices used in the exact raw
forest extraction. The property needed for direct marks is

  if selected carrier r has a retained mark y, then y and y+1 are in Sigma.
                                                               (3.1)
This is stronger than ordinary explicit-reference closure but weaker
than requiring every physical gap to be selected.

At the FIRST R[K] initialization, the raw forest has no marks. Its
nonquiet scan is native-only, and the known first-root scan/extraction
theorem selects EVERY member of a native family born at a selected row.
All newly marked heads lie earlier in that same family, so y+1<=r is
also selected. The unselected parallel singleton rows are long step-1
and cannot create a family. Thus (3.1) holds for the first extraction.

At the SECOND forest, the initial selected prefix is a contiguous prefix.
The full tail packet of R[K] consists of its final two complete native
families; every direct mark in either family refers inside that family.
The raw template preserves this internal-direct-mark property on each
remaining parallel live packet by whole-packet transport.

When a rightmost active packet is consumed, all its rows except the
controller are selected. This is a contiguous interval. For a mark in
one of these selected rows, its internal head satisfies y<r<=last-1,
so y+1 belongs to that interval as well. The final live packet minus
its cut controller has the same property.

The other newly selected rows are images of already selected source rows.
Every retained copied mark is all-high by section 2. If r is such a row,
the inductive selection includes old y+1, and y+1<=r<e. Hence its copied
physical successor is actually present and selected:
  f(y+1)=f(y)+1.
This proves (3.1) by induction on exactly N-1 forest copies. No adjacency
is inferred merely from a reindexed core or a finite computation.

4. Natural membership survives extraction and endpoint repair
--------------------------------------------------------------
Consider ANY actual pattern with direct natural marks, and a selection
satisfying (3.1), explicit-reference closure, and retaining the final
row and final implicit cap. For a selected carrier r with mark y:

  (a) Factor row y and its PHYSICAL next point y+1 are both retained.
      Therefore row y needs no implicit-endpoint repair. Its actual
      owner J_y stays unchanged. Its natural cutoff theta_(y+1) is the
      same actual ordinal before and after reindexing.

  (b) The carrier r may skip physical rows on its right. Write its old
      implicit cap c=theta_(r+1), and its next selected cap c'>=c.
      If c'>c, choose the established actual endpoint transporter G
      with crit(G)=c and G(c)=c', and set J'_r=G(J_r).
      This is the usual ordinary endpoint repair: named explicit edges
      and the critical point remain correct, and owners stay in Iter(j).

      Independently of that OPEN calculation, application gives the
      MEMBERSHIP equality J'_r equiv_c J_r. Since y<r,
        Delta=theta_(y+1)<=theta_r<c,
      downward weakening and transitivity with the old direct certificate
      yield
        J'_r equiv_Delta J_r equiv_Delta J_y.       (4.1)
      If c'=c there is no change and the assertion is immediate.

  (c) A row serving as any selected mark's factor cannot simultaneously
      need a repair, by (a). Thus all factor owners used in (4.1) remain
      unchanged even when carriers are repaired independently. There is
      no circular simultaneous-owner matching requirement.

This proves the precise natural membership extraction lemma needed here.
It does NOT prove arbitrary OPEN extraction preserves natural Delta.
Without (3.1), raising a factor's next point could raise the required
cutoff and the above argument would fail. The successor-closure hypothesis
is the essential newly checked feature of these two specific forests.
