2026-10-16. Penultimate shadow lines through an arbitrary finite raw forest
=========================================================================

Status: a conditional ALL-n transport theorem for the RAW forest, and hence
for QUIET original expansions. It proves the needed cap bound from the
owning live packet; it does not assume an arbitrary environment keeps the
old cap below C. No tests or descent runs were needed.

Dependencies read:
  local-budget2-initializer.txt, especially sections 9--12;
  local-budget-full-iblp.txt;
  old original-audit-root-forest-interface.txt;
  old original-audit-open-extraction-repair.txt.
The exact raw-forest syntactic identity and its conditional actual marked
copy semantics remain explicit premises, not reproved global legacy safety.

1. Data and the three different boundaries
------------------------------------------
Let P have r rows, final controller F, implicit cap c=theta_(r+1), and
final explicit point b=theta_r. Let
  e_idx = core_r[-L_r],  e=theta_(e_idx).
Thus [e_idx,r] is its complete original FOREST packet, cap included.
For its final owner one has F(e)=c. This e is the original raw-forest
boundary, NOT generally the first image s[L] of its full row.

The penultimate row r-1 has its own full-packet start
  p_idx = core_(r-1)[-L_(r-1)],  p=theta_(p_idx),
and implicit cap b. Suppose an actual M-line is supplied on
  (p,b,D), with b<D<c.                               (1.1)
Call it the penultimate shadow. Its root p need NOT be below e in the
generic theorem; it may be inside the last packet. It is always below b.

Separately, suppose the main ready resource is an actual m-line on
  (e,c,C), c<C<kappa4,                               (1.2)
with the closed-packet metadata that licenses the E packet supply.
All witnesses are in the same Iter(j). These two lines have different
roles and are not to be identified merely by comparing their lengths.

Choose a specified n with 2<=n<=2^m. The direct m-round doubling supplies
2^m parallel whole packets. Retain their FIRST n complete packets; let
C_n be their actual final cap, so C_n<=C. The first original packet and
its actual cap c are unchanged. In particular the shadow (1.1) is still
available in that first packet's final gap (b,c).

2. The live-forest invariant: track the FIRST, not the consumed last packet
------------------------------------------------------------------------
At every stage of the raw forest, discard none of the actual witnesses.
The syntactic raw-forest induction has an active contiguous source block
followed by h parallel live packets, h=n,n-1,...,1. Earlier frozen rows
may also remain. Their existence does not change the following facts:

  (a) The active controller is the last row of the RIGHTMOST live packet.
      If A is its actual owner and a is the FIRST point of that active
      packet (the current core[-L] boundary), then
                         A(a)=C_n.                  (2.1)

  (b) With h>=2, the ENTIRE first live packet is in the copied source
      interval. Its cap c_1 is at the next live packet's first point, so
                         c_1<=a.                    (2.2)
      Equality occurs when only two live packets remain.

  (c) A raw short copy replaces the active tail by the A-image of its
      source block and its first h-1 live packets. These packets remain
      contiguous and parallel. Their final cap is A(a)=C_n, unchanged.
      The first live packet in the next forest is the A-image of the old
      FIRST live packet, not the old rightmost packet with its cut owner.

These are the actual geometric counterparts of the established raw-forest
template. They use complete packets, including their implicit caps. Merely
translating cores without those edges would not justify (2.1)--(2.2).

3. Transport the first packet's penultimate shadow
--------------------------------------------------
Inductively retain actual data (p_1,b_1,D_1,c_1) and an M-line
  (p_1,b_1,D_1), b_1<D_1<c_1,
where b_1 is the explicit point of the first packet's controller and c_1
is that packet's implicit cap. The row immediately preceding that controller
is the current image of the original penultimate row.

For h>=2, apply the active owner A to every shadow-line witness. By (2.2),

  A(b_1)<A(D_1)<A(c_1)<=A(a)=C_n.                  (3.1)

Thus the next shadow is exactly
  (A(p_1), A(b_1), A(D_1)),
and its containing packet cap is A(c_1). This establishes the invariant
for the new FIRST live packet. No hypothesis A(c)<=C_n about the original
untransported c is used. The inequality follows from the CURRENT owning
packet's cap c_1<=a before each individual copy.

The shadow root is also correct: its coordinate is an explicit point of
the current penultimate row, so the raw forest's commuting address square
maps it to the exact central-tail boundary of that copied row. Its step
and core length are unchanged by a RAW copy. If p_1 lies below the copy
critical point it is fixed; otherwise it is genuinely moved. No blanket
assumption that every shadow root lies below e is necessary.

SINGLETON PACKET CASE. If the last packet has only its controller, the
penultimate row lies in the adjacent source block, not inside the packet.
It is nevertheless copied at the same stage: the active pivot precedes
that source-block last row. Its implicit cap is the first live packet's
start b_1, so the same transformed cap A(b_1) is obtained. The shadow's
third point lies inside the first packet's gap (b_1,c_1), and (3.1) applies
unchanged. This is why it is useful to track that row together with the
first packet, rather than require every penultimate row to be inside it.

There are exactly n-1 such raw short copies. Therefore the final shadow
is obtained by a known composite transport T=A_(n-1) ... A_1 and satisfies
  (T(p),T(b),T(D)) is M-lined,
  T(b)<T(D)<C_n.                                    (3.2)
The notation T is ordinary composition on points; witnesses are transported
by successive applications A_(n-1)(...(A_1(U))...). They stay in Iter(j).

4. The final cut and exact reference-closed extraction
------------------------------------------------------
After those n-1 copies, a single live working packet remains. The final
work-controller cut removes its last row. The actual final row is its
penultimate row, namely the cumulative image of original row r-1. Its
implicit cap is T(b), not C_n and not an arbitrary later selected point.
Its full-packet start is T(p). Thus (3.2) already has the EXACT two endpoints
needed to supply the next raw full packet:

               new packet start = T(p),
               new implicit cap = T(b),
               renewed outer cap = T(D)<C_n<=C.     (4.1)

The raw forest's reference-closed selection retains this final actual row
and its final cap. Hence this final owner is NOT repaired. Repairs of
earlier retained owners do not move any selected actual point or alter
the independently retained shadow witnesses. They cannot replace T(b)
by an unverified gap endpoint. This is exactly the final-cap clause of
original-audit-open-extraction-repair.txt section 3.

By the established raw-forest lemma, the extracted syntax is precisely
  cut(FullCopy^(n-1)(P)).
Consequently (4.1) is the exact interface of its final raw row, not merely
an interface of some larger unselected forest.

5. Scope after the ORIGINAL frozen scan
---------------------------------------
If the original final scan is QUIET, it changes nothing. In that case the
above theorem supplies an actual M-line at the precise next packet start
and implicit cap of ORIGINAL P[n], with renewed outer cap strictly below
the old one. This is an all-n result, not only the n=2 instance checked in
local-budget2-initializer.txt.

The actual-copy and old-mark safety premises are still required. For a
closed quiet q-ribbon/ancestry cone, use the existing quiet legacy theorem;
for general P, do not infer safety from this shadow-line calculation.

If the scan is NOT quiet, this file does NOT identify the final packet
start with T(p). Even a proper mark event can add points and change the
step; one must re-evaluate core[-L] in the final native/marked family.
Long cores with early target marks are a specific case requiring care.
It is invalid to say "the cap is preserved, so the required starting
endpoint is also preserved." A further scan-port ledger is needed.

There is one directly proved useful exception: a scan containing only
NATIVE completion and no effective inherited-mark event. In a native event
at row i, write its old consecutive central points as p0,e0, old step L,
and record size t. Every source point inserted by the native q traversal
lies strictly between p0 and e0, and every new target coordinate is among
i+1,...,i+t, hence after e0. These two sets are disjoint from the old core.
The final/top native row has exactly 2t new points and step L+t. If the
old core length was h, the old e0 position was h-L; its new position is
h-L+t=(h+2t)-(L+t). Therefore the top row's core[-(L+t)] is precisely the
same actual e0. Its implicit cap is also unchanged by native interpolation.
Earlier native insertions only rename old actual points, preserving this
identity. Thus the final shadow endpoints survive a NATIVE-ONLY scan.

For the FIRST original expansion R[n], this exception applies for every n:
R and its raw copies have no inherited marks; newly generated families
are skipped by the frozen scan, so no generated star causes another mark
event in that same scan. The exact first-root scan/forest theorem supplies
its actual native semantics. Hence the generic raw shadow theorem extends
to the full original R[n], not merely its unscanned buffer.

6. What this buys for numerical updates, and what it does not
-------------------------------------------------------------
If the supplied shadow length M is at least m+1, then after the exact
original quiet macro one may retain or directly compress to m+1 segments
at the next correct endpoints. This gives the requested STRICT increase
of the usable linedness parameter in a prescribed finite macro. There is
no unknown M-until-special-shape normalization:
  m specified E rounds, n-1 specified raw short copies, final cut,
  explicit extraction, and the quiet scan.

However, this theorem PRESERVES M; it does not itself enlarge the shadow
length. A fixed enormous M only pays finitely many chosen +1 renewals.
To close an unlimited recursive Hardy compiler, the next state needs its
OWN penultimate shadow with sufficient new length, as well as its main
ready line. Keeping only (4.1) loses that second resource. A complete ledger
of deeper-row shadows, or an actual fresh-shadow generation theorem, is
still necessary. This is a concrete remaining invariant, not an endpoint
ambiguity in the one-step transport proved here.
