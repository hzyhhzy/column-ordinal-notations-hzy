5. Remaining typed gap, with an explicit direction test
------------------------------------------------------
The availability of the fan in section 4 is not a complete recursive
type stack. Suppose a previous scalar operator has admission beta_i
and output exit d_i, and the next helper is w_(i+1)>d_i>beta_i.
The next zero-copy actor has critical point w_(i+1), hence FIXES beta_i.
After the first whole-function evaluation, the new argument can have
exit greater than beta_i, while the copied base operator still admits
only inputs of exit <=beta_i. It cannot be called on that argument.

The established high type-1 seed avoided this failure by an independent
promoter J: its scalar admission J(a) was ABOVE the helper w1, while
its output J(c) was BELOW the first payload left E(a). A similar fitting
base-box contract must be supplied here; the inequalities d_i<w_(i+1)
alone do not supply it. Neither a root certificate for C[k] nor arbitrary
finite height of a compatible fan settles this whole-function domain
condition.

The exact published identity mu=gamma256 guarantees t_i>mu after at
most 257 discarded Qi segments. This removes the finite lower-threshold
issue in section 4, but does not repair the typed admission direction.
