1. A stronger strict-cap lemma
-----------------------------
Exactly A=(cA o cA)(K), and (cA o cA)(mu)=a. Hence
  A(<a)=(cA o cA)(K(<mu)).
The point mu is regular, and K(mu)=a>mu. The supremum K(<mu) is the
supremum of mu ordinals below the regular cardinal a, so K(<mu)<a.
Strict monotonicity and the fact that a is a limit ordinal now imply
  A(<a)<(cA o cA)(<a)=(K o K)(v)<theta8<kappa4.       (1)
The last equality and bound were proved in section 4 of the preceding
low-fan-staircase note. In particular both A(mu) and A(theta5) are
actual generated critical points strictly below theta8. No claim is
made about A(a): in fact A(a)>kappa4, consistent with (1).
