# External inputs and attribution / 外部文献

These are cited inputs, not vendored papers. Bibliographic names and versions were checked against primary arXiv records for this edition.

1. **Renrui Qi, Notes on Laver Tables, v5.** [Record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.06733v5) · [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.06733v5). Supplies ordinary BLP/BLS definitions, finite-parameter linedness, the standard five-row realization, the actual long-line construction and the critical-count/period interface. The local mark, parent-packet and typed-BMS extensions remain separate arguments.

2. **Randall Dougherty, Critical points in an algebra of elementary embeddings.** [Record](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9205202) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9205202). Finite point tables, membership-truncation algebra and strict-sup comparisons used in the manuscripts. Journal version: *Annals of Pure and Applied Logic* 65 (1993).

3. **Randall Dougherty, Critical points in an algebra of elementary embeddings, II.** [Record](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9503204) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9503204). Standard finite lines, endpoint extension and critical-point counting interfaces.

4. **Rachel Hunter, Well-Orderedness of the Bashicu Matrix System, v3.** [Record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.04606v3) · [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.04606v3). Standard BMS well-ordering and parent-table replication, especially Lemma 2.5. The current arXiv author name is Rachel Hunter; older drafts may use an earlier attribution. Quantitative nonstandard covers and the forest compiler here are additional local arguments.

5. **Astra–Qi IBLP well-foundedness manuscript.** [Author-hosted PDF](https://github.com/QiRenrui/basic-laver-pattern/blob/main/IBLP_wellfoundedness_en.pdf). A version-specific rule reference for the short-tail correspondence. The legacy Python edition is separately pinned; no unproved general legacy/paper guard equivalence is imported.

Steel's bounded-application theorem is used through the formulation cited in Qi's paper. It is not reproved here. The finite integer potential for total short-modify counts is proved locally.

## Local source editions

Selected research manuscripts retain their original Chinese or English mathematical exposition. The three main papers identify the actual interfaces and derive the natural-input versions.

The user-supplied original Python kernel is retained byte for byte as [iblp_original.py](code/iblp_original.py) to pin the rule version and reproduce bounded identities. Original contact text is retained; this collection assigns no new authorship or license. Its hashes are recorded in [SOURCE_MANIFEST.json](SOURCE_MANIFEST.json). The third paper defines its paper-guard transition mathematically; the legacy kernel is not relabelled as that transition engine.

No external PDFs, Lean projects, private chat exports or large experiment outputs are bundled.
